# Divisions

## Overview

The game is structured around climbing through 3 divisions. You start in Division 3 and work your way up to Division 1. Winning Division 1 completes the game.

## Structure

- **Division 3** - Starting division. Placing in the bottom 2 renders GAME OVER (you're out of the division!)
- **Division 2** - Unlocked by being top2 in Div 3 playoffs
- **Division 1** - Unlocked by being top2 in Div 2 playoffs

## Season Flow

Each division plays out as a full season:
- 8 regular season games (home & away vs 4 opponents)
- Top 4 teams qualify for the finals (semi-final + final)
- Being in the finals (so, top 2) promotes you to the next division
- Bottom 2 teams go down a division

## Progression

- Players carry over between divisions with their XP and levels
- ~35 minutes per season, ~1h45m for a full playthrough (Div 3 to champion)

## Promotion & Relegation

- Top 2 teams: promote to the next division
- Bottom 2: Relegation to lower division (relegation from divison 3 = GAME OVER). If an AI team is relegated,
  below division 3, they're removed and a new team is created.
- The rest (so, just 1 team in the middle assuming 5 teams): replay the same division next season

## Winning the Game

Winning the Division 1 final completes the game. This is the ultimate goal of franchise mode.

## Difficulty Scaling

The opponent teams have higher base stats in higher divisions.
